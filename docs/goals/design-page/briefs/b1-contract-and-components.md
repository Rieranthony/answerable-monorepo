## What done looks like

1. **The stylesheet runs on the contract.** `packages/ui/src/styles/globals.css` imports `./tokens.css`, drops its hand-written light `:root` block (tokens.css replaces it), keeps `--mosaic-mark` and `--font-system` as library-owned variables, and rewrites `.dark` so every variable reads its hook with the dark default (`--background: var(--design-background, oklch(0 0 0))`, and so on for the whole set, including the new roles: a dark value for overlay, surface-dark, on-dark, on-accent, success, warning, info, highlight-1 to 4, chart-1 to 5, keeping the accents themselves). The `@theme inline` block maps every contract token Tailwind can use:
   - colours: the shadcn set as today plus `--color-overlay`, `--color-surface-dark`, `--color-on-dark`, `--color-on-accent`, `--color-success`, `--color-warning`, `--color-info`, `--color-highlight-1` to `-4`, `--color-chart-1` to `-5`;
   - `--spacing: var(--spacing-unit)`; `--radius-*` from `--radius` as today plus `--radius-full: var(--radius-full)`;
   - `--shadow-xs|sm|md|lg: var(--elevation-xs|sm|md|lg)` and `--shadow-none: var(--elevation-none)`;
   - `--font-weight-body|label|statement: var(--weight-body|label|statement)` (giving `font-body`, `font-label`, `font-statement`);
   - `--text-xs|sm|base|lg` and their `--line-height` bound to the badge, ui-text, body-md and body-lg levels (`--text-sm: var(--type-ui-text-size)`, `--text-sm--line-height: var(--type-ui-text-leading)`, …);
   - motion: `--default-transition-duration: var(--duration-base)`, `--default-transition-timing-function: var(--ease)`, `--ease-design: var(--ease)`, and utilities `duration-fast|base|slow` through `@utility`;
   - `@utility focus-ring { box-shadow: var(--focus-ring) }` for `focus-visible:focus-ring`.
   Compile once and look at the output (`@tailwindcss/node`'s `compile` from `apps/web`, as the probe in findings.md did) to confirm utilities emit `var(--…)` and nothing self-references. `--font-sans` stays `@theme inline { --font-sans: var(--font-sans) }`: tokens.css declares the default and `next/font` supplies the self-hosted face through `--design-font-sans` (B3 wires that).
2. **The components exist and follow the file.** Add from the base-nova registry, into `packages/ui/src/components`: badge, card, tabs, switch, checkbox, radio-group, select, slider, dialog, tooltip, popover, dropdown-menu, table, alert, progress, skeleton, avatar, separator, textarea, toggle-group, kbd, label, scroll-area, toast. (`apps/web/node_modules/.bin/shadcn add <item>` from `apps/web` routes into `packages/ui`; it rewrites `cn` imports to a bare `cn` and with `--overwrite` replaces button and input, so fix imports to `@answerable/ui/lib/utils` and keep ours. The registry item list is in findings.md.) Then restyle every component, old and new, to the `components` section of `DESIGN.md` through the contract:
   - no borders or outlines anywhere: fills (`bg-input`, `bg-secondary`, `bg-muted`), elevation (`shadow-xs|sm|md|lg`) and separators (`border-b border-border` on rows and groups only) define shape; the only ring is the focus ring (`focus-visible:focus-ring`), on every interactive element;
   - radius through the token: `rounded-lg` (which is `var(--radius)`), never a literal `rounded-none`; radio and avatar use `rounded-full`;
   - type through the levels: `type-button`, `type-label`, `type-badge`, `type-ui-text`, `type-caption`, `type-table-head`, `type-eyebrow`, `type-heading-h2` (dialog titles), `type-heading-h3` (card titles), `type-numeric` on numbers; weights only through `font-body|label|statement`, never `font-medium` or `font-semibold`;
   - sizes through the tokens: control heights `h-(--control-sm|md|lg|xl)` (32, 36, 40, 48px), menu items 32px, sidebar items 36px, nav 56px;
   - hover and pressed states that work for any injected design: `color-mix(in oklab, var(--input), var(--foreground) 6%)` for hover on fills and 12% for pressed, `hover:bg-accent` for ghost and menu items, `hover:opacity-90` on accent and destructive fills, a 2px underline with 4px offset on links;
   - button: variants `default` (primary fill), `secondary`, `ghost`, `link`, `destructive` (destructive fill, on-accent text), `accent` (a `highlight` prop 1 to 4 picking `bg-highlight-n`, on-accent text, 48px tall, 24px padding), sizes `sm | default | lg | xl | icon | icon-sm | icon-lg`;
   - input, textarea, select: `bg-input`, no border, hover deepens, focus switches to `bg-background` and the ring; invalid state is a 4px square `bg-destructive` marker beside the label and helper text in `text-foreground`, never coloured text (`FieldError` and `FieldLabel` carry this); `inputClassName` keeps its name and export (ID's login page imports it);
