## Audit A (deletion model), 2026-10-19, in the scratchpad `audits/a.md`

- Deletion machinery is 39% of the FKs (26 live of 66), 35% of the CHECKs, 39% of the triggers (probe 01).
- Hard delete refusals as owner (probe 03): users, organizations, members by `grant_contexts` restrict; clients by entitlements' restrict FK; resources by links, entitlements, `system_bindings`; the other 9 tables delete plainly or cascade. Physical purge of an erased person is impossible while their grant contexts exist.
- Design (ii′) recommended: hard delete accounts, groups, group_members, entitlements, organization_domains, sso_providers, oauth_client_resources, oauth_consents, organization_capabilities; children keep the live FK through `live boolean generated always as (true) stored` (probe 09: tombstoning a parent with a live child refused; a new child under a tombstoned parent refused; writers cannot set it). Removes 9 `deleted_at`, 9 triggers, 15 CHECKs, 3 live FKs, 1 live unique, 7 partial uniques, 61 of 129 `isNull(deletedAt)` filters; the two NND indexes move into Drizzle (`unique().nullsNotDistinct()`, probe 07).
- Erasure (probe 06): after `user.erased` the row keeps `name` and the original address in `retired_email`, 2 account tombstones keep sub and oid, re-sign-in refused `user_disabled` (erasure = permanent ban); after `organization.erased` 6 of 6 people keep email, name, a live account with encrypted upstream tokens and a session, 0 memberships. Fix: scrub name/image/retired_email under `users_deleted_check`, hard-delete accounts, erase orphaned people in organisation erasure; audit IP/user agent need a retention period.
- Live keys: +16–32 µs per insert, +2.5–8 µs per update (probe 04); 50 of 50 child inserts in 20–30 ms during a 2.5 s parent update (probe 05); generated parent `live` + any BEFORE UPDATE trigger → 2,466 ms waits on 17.9 (keep the plain column).
- `users.email` unique: keep (Better Auth `findUserByEmail` takes the first match, `link-account.mjs:62`; `email_conflict` fail-closed, 6 of 6 tests); relax `users_retired_email_check` to an implication so erasure can clear it.
- Open: erasure versus ban (owner); organisation erasure of ~200 people in one command not measured; IdP replacement under one domain not run (fixture fixes trusted origins); the admin MCP's slug precondition reads live organisations only (a tombstoned slug passes prepare, fails commit 409; source-read).
