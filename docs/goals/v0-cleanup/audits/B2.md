Estimated production lines: about 650 removed, 120 added (B2-31 is 21 of them, in area C). No SQL injection, no secret in a log or error body, no post-fetch organisation filtering and no soft-deleted row returned as live were found in scope (section 5).

**Dead query functions.** None. An import-based check (every `export` in `src/db` and `src/operations` against every non-test file outside `src/__tests__`, resolving named and namespace imports) found a production importer for all 106 query functions; knip's silence is correct here, not an artefact of the test re-exports. Only types are unused (B2-19).

**The five changes that matter most**

1. **Erasure clones into three helpers (B2-4, B2-5).** The baseline's two largest ID clones (60 and 35 lines) are the same soft-delete-and-capture statements written out per erasure path: entitlements in 4 places, group assignments in 4, members in 2, tokens and consents in 2. Helpers beside `groupAssignmentEvidence` (already in `groups.ts`) and `revokeTokens` (already in `oauth-tokens.ts`) remove about 170 lines and give one evidence shape per table.
2. **`relations.ts`: 12 of 17 relations have no reader (B2-1).** Better Auth's adapter needs only session→user, account→user, user→accounts, member→user/organization and organization→members (it runs `db.query` because `auth.ts:186` sets `joins: true`); nothing else calls `db.query` outside two tests that assert the relation graph itself. About 180 lines.
3. **Optional-filter helpers (B2-6, B2-7, B2-8).** 36 `x === undefined ? undefined : eq(column, x)` ternaries in 10 query files, 6 copies of the two-column `ilike` search, and `audit.ts` re-implementing `beforeCursor` and `cursorPage` twice. `optionalEq` and `contains` next to `beforeCursor` remove about 145 lines and give LIKE-wildcard escaping one home.
4. **Soft-deletion predicates (B2-2, B2-3, B2-12).** Two spellings (`sql\`${t.deletedAt} is null\`` 165 times in 24 files, `isNull(t.deletedAt)` 19 times in 8): pick `isNull`. And 31 predicates in scope filter a parent that a live foreign key already keeps live (3 of them repeat a predicate already in the same statement).
5. **`effectiveGrants` without a session (B2-13).** Both production callers pass a session; the optional-session path, which evaluates every membership and skips the tenant-authentication check, is reached only by 5 test calls. Making `sessionId` required removes a weaker policy path that nothing needs.
