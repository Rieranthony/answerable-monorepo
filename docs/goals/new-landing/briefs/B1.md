### 5. The splash (D2, D3)

`apps/web/components/new/splash.tsx`, rendered once in `(new)/layout.tsx` above `{children}`, so it plays on every full load and reload and never on in-page interaction. It must work with no animation library: CSS keyframes in `app/globals.css` (scoped class names `.splash-*`, inside `@layer components`) plus a small client effect.

**Colour.** `--splash` is one of `var(--highlight-1)`…`var(--highlight-4)` at random. Pick it before first paint with a tiny inline script rendered as the first child of `<body>` in the layout: it sets `document.documentElement.style.setProperty("--splash", "var(--highlight-" + (1 + Math.floor(Math.random() * 4)) + ")")`. Put `suppressHydrationWarning` on `<html>`. The CSS default (no JS) is `var(--highlight-1)`.

**Drawing.** A `fixed inset-0 z-50` element containing one full-viewport SVG. A nested `<svg x="50%" y="50%" overflow="visible">` puts the origin at the viewport centre. The colour field is a `<rect width="100%" height="100%" fill="var(--splash)" mask="url(#splash-mask)">`; the mask is a white full rect plus a black comma (the hole) in a group scaled from 0. Scale the comma so its 48-unit square head is 64 px on screen (scale 4/3), centred on the head square's centre (1128, 240).

**Sequence** (CSS animation delays from first paint; total about 2.2 s):

1. 0–200 ms: the field only.
2. 200–900 ms: three white squares (64 px, the comma head's size, gap 32 px, centred as a row, the third one where the comma head will be) appear one after another, 200 ms apart, each fading and scaling from 0.6 to 1 in 160 ms.
3. 900–1300 ms: the first two squares fade out; the third grows its tail: draw the white comma over the third square and reveal its tail with a `clip-path` inset animating from the head's bottom edge down to the full comma (the comma path is the square plus the tail, so it reads as one shape growing). The row recentres on the comma (translate) in the same 400 ms.
4. 1300–2200 ms: the reveal. The comma hole in the mask scales from 0 to the white comma's size instantly at 1300 ms (white comma stays on top), then the white comma fades out over 160 ms while the hole and anything in its group scale up from 1× to 60× around the head's centre with `cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.2, 1)`, so the page appears inside the growing comma and the field leaves the screen.
5. End: the overlay's final keyframe is `visibility: hidden`, so the page is usable without JS. With JS, remove the overlay from the DOM on the last `animationend`, with a 3 s timeout fallback.

Use `transform-box: fill-box` / `transform-origin` on SVG groups so CSS transforms scale around the right point; check the result in your head against the viewBox maths, then the coordinator checks it in a browser.

**Skip.** A click, tap or key press during the splash fades the overlay out in 200 ms and removes it.

**Reduced motion.** `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` hides the overlay entirely (`display: none`).

**Accessibility.** The overlay is `aria-hidden="true"`; the page under it is in the DOM and readable from the first byte. No focus trap.
